## Primary sources and boundaries

- [Pulsar timing paper, full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18329v2): read
  methods and validation appendices. The useful examples are the non-Gaussian
  signal, normalizing flows for compressed amplitudes, diffusion surrogates for
  timing residuals, and consistent cubic compression. No blanket claim of being
  the first correct model is made. The diffusion results are sensitivity forecasts
  under noise-only assumptions, not arbitrary-signal calibration guarantees.
- [Antinuclei paper, full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12314v2): read signal
  modeling, showering/coalescence calibration, and conclusions. This grounds the
  importance of physical modeling; the new Perspective does not claim an AMS
  detection, a quantitative fit to the candidates, or an ML result in this paper.
- [IceCube paper, full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.06734v2): read signal,
  backgrounds, approximate detector sensitivity, and model constraints. It is a
  forecast, not a completed detector Monte Carlo analysis or detection.

P–P/calibration checks come from Fabrizio's current instructions and are framed
as a methodological commitment, not as a test claimed to appear in the PTA
paper. Coverage language describes ensembles of simulated data and does not
claim that an aggregate check proves calibration at every parameter value.

The unpublished Galactic-center proposal and its hypothesized explanation are
absent from the public copy. No preliminary research results are invented. The
Q-ball publication remains available but is not forced into an unrelated example.

Illustrations are decorative conceptual SVGs, not measured plots or reported
calibration results. Their meaning is conveyed by the adjacent text, and they
are hidden from assistive technologies to avoid redundant descriptions.
